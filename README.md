# Healthcare Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI healthcare analytics project designed to turn patient and hospital records into clear, actionable insights.

The project looks at healthcare data from three perspectives: **individual patient journeys, overall patient patterns, and hospital & financial operations**.

---

##  Project Overview

Healthcare datasets can contain large amounts of patient, treatment, hospital, and billing information, making it difficult to quickly identify meaningful patterns.

For this project, I transformed a raw healthcare dataset into an interactive Power BI dashboard that allows users to explore patient information, admission patterns, medical conditions, test outcomes, hospital activity, and billing.

The goal was to move from raw records to a dashboard that supports faster understanding of healthcare operations and patient-level information.

---

##  Type of Analytics

**Descriptive Analytics + Diagnostic Analytics**

The project focuses primarily on understanding what happened in the dataset and exploring patterns that may help explain differences across patients, conditions, hospitals, admissions, and financial activity.

---

##  Business Problem

Healthcare records contain valuable information, but raw data is difficult to interpret at a glance.

The key challenge was to create a solution that could help users:

* Understand individual patient information
* Explore patient demographics and medical conditions
* Monitor admission activity
* Examine recorded test outcomes
* Compare hospital activity
* Understand billing patterns
* Explore the data interactively rather than relying on static tables

---

##  Business Questions

The dashboard was designed to answer questions such as:

* How many patients are represented in the dataset?
* What is the average patient age?
* Which medical condition appears most frequently?
* What are the recorded test-result patterns?
* How many admissions are recorded?
* How many hospitals are represented?
* What is the total and average billing amount?
* What information can be identified about an individual patient's journey?
* How do hospital and financial measures change when the data is filtered?

---

##  Approach

I worked through the project in several stages:

### 1. Data Preparation

I reviewed and prepared the healthcare dataset for analysis using Power Query.

### 2. Data Modeling

I structured the data for Power BI analysis and created a calendar table to support time-based analysis.

### 3. DAX Development

I created measures for KPIs, patient information, billing, admissions, test-result analysis, and time-based comparisons.

### 4. Dashboard Development

I designed an interactive dashboard where filters and patient selections dynamically update the displayed information.

### 5. Data Storytelling

I organized the dashboard into three analytical areas:

* **Patient Profile**
* **Patient Insight**
* **Hospital & Financial Operations**

---

##  Dashboard Pages

### 1. Patient Profile

Provides a focused view of an individual patient, including:

* Patient name
* Age
* Gender
* Medical condition
* Blood type
* Hospital
* Admission type
* Room number
* Medication
* Admission date
* Discharge date
* Patient journey timeline

The timeline follows the patient's journey from **Admission → Treatment → Testing → Discharge**.

---

### 2. Patient Insight

Provides a broader view of the patient population through measures such as:

* Total patients
* Average age
* Most common medical condition
* Test-result patterns
* Patient trends
* Admission activity

This page moves the analysis from an individual patient to the overall population.

---

### 3. Hospital & Financial Operations

Focuses on operational and financial measures including:

* Total hospitals
* Total admissions
* Total billing amount
* Average billing amount
* Hospital activity
* Financial patterns

This provides an additional perspective beyond individual patient records.

---

##  Key Insights

The dashboard makes it possible to explore:

* Patient demographics and medical conditions
* The distribution of recorded test outcomes
* Admission activity over time
* Differences in hospital activity
* Overall billing patterns
* Individual patient journeys from admission to discharge

The interactive design also allows users to filter the data and investigate these patterns from different perspectives.

> **Important:** The dashboard is an analytical exploration of the dataset. The findings should not be interpreted as clinical diagnoses or evidence of causation.

---

##  Business Value

The project demonstrates how healthcare records can be transformed into an interactive analytical solution.

Instead of navigating through raw records, users can quickly move between:

**Patient → Population → Hospital → Financial Operations**

This creates a more connected view of the healthcare data and makes important metrics easier to explore.
